# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS on GitHub Pages (jpetzke.github.io), no build step. Decided during the 2026-11 redesign: drop Bootstrap 3, jQuery, Modernizr, FontAwesome and the Tooplate leftovers; hand-written CSS, no JS libraries.

## Users

People who look Jonas Petzke up: professional contacts, colleagues, potential clients or employers, fellow students. They arrive from a link or a search, spend under a minute, and want to know who he is, what he does now, and how to reach him. The page works as a general personal card, not a job application.

## Product Purpose

A personal card on the web for Jonas Petzke. Success: a visitor leaves knowing his current role, what he works on, one or two personal facts, and a way to get in touch — and thinks "okay, nice."

## Positioning

Real, current facts about one person, written by him, in English. No generic self-praise; facts replace taglines.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: English only.
- Pages: home (`index.html`) and Quick Links (`quicklinks.html`, a short curated list of things he uses; may be folded into home).
- Privacy is a hard constraint: no home town or residence, no Instagram, no chess club link, no newspaper scan, no photos of other people. City-level location is not shown.
- Contact: email `jonas.petzke02@gmail.com` (already public via this repo's history), LinkedIn (`https://www.linkedin.com/in/jonas-petzke`), GitHub (`https://github.com/jpetzke`).

## Product Principles

1. Facts over adjectives: every line states something true and specific.
2. Privacy by default: show what helps a visitor reach him, nothing that locates him.
3. Current first: what he does now leads; 2022 is history.
4. Source is part of the card: lean, modern, hand-written code a developer would be fine with others viewing.
